# Logo & Favicon

## Logo

Upload your brand logo in **Theme Settings → Logo**.

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Logo image** | Your brand logo (SVG or PNG recommended) |
| **Logo width** | Logo display width: 50–250px (default 120px) |

### Logo Recommendations
- **Format:** SVG for sharp scaling, or PNG with transparent background
- **Size:** 400×200px minimum for retina displays
- **File size:** Under 100KB

## Favicon

Upload a favicon in **Theme Settings → Favicon**. This appears in browser tabs.

- **Size:** 32×32px
- **Format:** PNG
- Use your brand icon or a simplified version of your logo
