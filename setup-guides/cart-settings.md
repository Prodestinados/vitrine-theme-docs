# Cart Settings

Configure cart behavior in **Theme Settings → Cart**.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Cart type** | Choose between **Cart drawer** (slide-out panel) or **Cart page** (full page) |

## Cart Drawer

The cart drawer slides in from the right when a customer adds a product. It shows cart items, quantities, subtotal, and a checkout button without leaving the current page.

## Cart Page

A dedicated full-page cart with line items, order notes, and checkout button.

## Tips

- Cart drawer is recommended for most stores — it keeps customers on the page
- The cart page is better for stores with complex products or order note requirements
