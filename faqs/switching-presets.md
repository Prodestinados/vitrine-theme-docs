# Switching Presets

## How do I switch between Atelier, Runway, and Salon?

Go to the **Theme Editor** and look for the preset selector in **Theme Settings**. Selecting a different preset changes:

- Typography (heading and body fonts)
- Color schemes (all 5 schemes)
- Layout settings (page width, spacing, border radius)

**Your content is not affected** — section text, images, products, and page structure remain the same.

> **Tip:** Preview each preset before saving to see which aesthetic fits your brand best.
