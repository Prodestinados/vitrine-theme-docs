# Choosing a Preset

Atelier ships with three curated presets. Each offers a complete visual identity — typography, colors, spacing, and layout — ready to use out of the box.

## Preset Comparison

| | Atelier | Runway | Salon |
|---|---------|--------|-------|
| **Mood** | Warm neutral minimalism | Bold editorial streetwear | Elegant refined luxury |
| **Headings** | DM Sans Bold | Bebas Neue | Cormorant |
| **Body** | DM Sans Regular | Work Sans | Lato |
| **Page Width** | 1200px | 1400px | 1000px |
| **Button Style** | Square corners | Square corners | Fully rounded |
| **Accent** | Warm browns | Bold red | Muted gold |

## How to Switch Presets

1. Open the **Theme Editor** (Customize)
2. Click the **gear icon** at the bottom of the sidebar
3. Under **Theme settings**, look for the preset selector
4. Choose your preset and click **Save**

> **Note:** Switching presets changes global settings (fonts, colors, spacing). Section content like text and images is not affected.

## Which Preset Should You Use?

- **Atelier** — Best for contemporary fashion, clean brands, and warm aesthetics
- **Runway** — Best for streetwear, sneaker culture, and bold editorial looks
- **Salon** — Best for beauty, jewelry, and luxury lifestyle brands
