## Get Help

We typically respond within **1–2 business days**.

**Submit a support request:** [Open Support Form](https://tally.so/r/vG2kP8)

When submitting a request, please include:

- Your store URL (e.g., `yourstore.myshopify.com`)
- Which preset you're using (Atelier, Runway, or Salon)
- A description of the issue or question
- A screenshot if the issue is visual (optional)
