# Newsletter

An email signup section connected to Shopify's built-in customer marketing.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Subheading** | Small text above heading |
| **Heading** | Section heading (default: "Stay in the loop") |
| **Heading size** | Small, Medium, Large |
| **Text** | Description text |
| **Text alignment** | Left or Center |
| **Color scheme** | Scheme 1–5 (default: 3) |

## How It Works

Subscribers are added to your Shopify customer list with email marketing consent. Manage subscribers in **Shopify Admin → Customers**.

## Tips

- Defaults to Color Scheme 3 (dark) for visual contrast — makes it stand out
- Place near the bottom of your homepage, above the footer
- Keep the copy short and benefit-focused
