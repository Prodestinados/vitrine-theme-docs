# Product Recommendations

Displays related products on product pages. Powered by Shopify's recommendation engine.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Heading** | Section heading (default: "You may also like") |
| **Heading size** | Small or Medium |
| **Products to show** | 2–8 products (default 4) |
| **Columns** | 3 or 4 (default 4) |
| **Image ratio** | Portrait or Square |
| **Color scheme** | Scheme 1–5 |

## How It Works

Shopify automatically selects related products based on purchase history, product descriptions, and collections. You don't need to manually curate recommendations.

## Tips

- This section only appears on **product pages**
- Use portrait ratio to match your product grid
- 4 products in 4 columns is the most common layout
