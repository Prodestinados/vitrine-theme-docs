# Login & Registration

Vitrine includes styled pages for customer login, registration, and password recovery.

## Pages

- **Login** — Email + password form with "Forgot password?" link
- **Register** — First name, last name, email, password
- **Password Reset** — Email-based password recovery

## Enabling Customer Accounts

In **Shopify Admin → Settings → Checkout**:
- **Accounts are optional** — Customers can checkout as guests
- **Accounts are required** — Customers must log in to checkout

## Tips

- Most stores use "optional" to reduce checkout friction
- The login link appears in the header when accounts are enabled
