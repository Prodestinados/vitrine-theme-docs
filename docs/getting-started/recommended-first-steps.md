# Recommended First Steps

After installing Vitrine, follow this checklist to get your store looking great.

## Quick Setup Checklist

1. **Choose your preset** — Vitrine, Runway, or Salon ([guide](choosing-a-preset.md))
2. **Upload your logo** — Theme Settings → Logo & Favicon ([guide](../setup-guides/logo-and-favicon.md))
3. **Set up navigation** — Create your main menu and footer links ([guide](../setup-guides/navigation-and-menus.md))
4. **Configure your hero** — Add a lifestyle image and compelling headline ([guide](../sections-guide/hero-banner.md))
5. **Add products** — Upload product images and descriptions in Shopify Admin
6. **Set up a Featured Collection** — Showcase your best products ([guide](../sections-guide/featured-collection.md))
7. **Add social links** — Connect your Instagram, TikTok, etc. ([guide](../setup-guides/social-media-links.md))
8. **Review on mobile** — Use the Theme Editor's mobile preview to check responsiveness
9. **Save and publish** — When ready, set Vitrine as your live theme
