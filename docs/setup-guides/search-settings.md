# Search Settings

Vitrine includes predictive search that shows results as customers type.

## How It Works

1. Customer clicks the search icon in the header
2. A search overlay appears
3. As they type, products, collections, pages, and articles appear in real-time

## Search Results

The search results page shows filtered results with:
- Product cards with images and prices
- Pagination for large result sets

## Tips

- Ensure your products have descriptive titles and tags for better search results
- The search icon appears in the header by default
